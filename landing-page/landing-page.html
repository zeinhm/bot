FAQ

Questions, answered.

The essentials before you connect an account. Still curious? Spin up a paper account — it costs nothing.

What is ZENITH Bot?

ZENITH is an automated trading bot that executes a single, backtested strategy — Accumulation–Manipulation–Distribution with Fair Value Gap confirmation — on Binance Futures. It watches BTC, ETH and SOL on the 15-minute timeframe and places trades only when the structure confirms, with the stop loss and take profit set on the exchange the moment it enters. You connect your own account; the bot handles the discipline so there's no second-guessing, no revenge trading, and no skipped setups.

How does the strategy work?

The bot scans for periods where price coils into a tight range relative to recent volatility — accumulation. It then watches for a sharp wick that sweeps beyond the range, trapping breakout traders and hunting stops — manipulation. Once the trap is set, it waits for a Fair Value Gap to form in the opposite direction, confirming the real move, and enters at the FVG candle close.

Every trade targets a 2:1 reward-to-risk ratio: the stop sits beyond the manipulation extreme, and take profit is twice that distance. The win rate is intentionally below 50% — the edge comes from the payoff, not from being right most of the time. Liquidity-sweep, trend and seasonal filters sit out the weakest conditions, and position size scales down automatically during losing streaks.

Why Binance Futures?

Binance USDT-M perpetuals give the deepest liquidity and tightest spreads on BTC, ETH and SOL, which matters for a strategy that enters and exits with market orders. Server-side stop-loss and take-profit orders execute even if the bot or your connection drops, and the API exposes the real fill and funding data the dashboard reconciles against — so every number you see is pulled from the exchange, not self-reported.

Is it secure?

Your funds never leave your Binance account — ZENITH only places trades. API keys are encrypted at rest, and you create them with Futures trading enabled and withdrawals disabled, locked to our server IPs. You sign in with Google, and optional two-factor authentication gates sensitive actions like changing keys or bot settings. We can't move your money, and you can revoke access from Binance at any time.

Who is this for?

Traders who believe in a rules-based edge but struggle to execute it without emotion. If you want a transparent, backtested system that runs the same way every time — and you'd rather watch the logic than babysit a chart — ZENITH is built for you. Start on paper with $10k in virtual funds, watch it trade against live prices, and go live only when you're convinced. Crypto futures carry real risk; trade only what you can afford to lose.
