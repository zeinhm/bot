How it works

Not a black box. A rules-based edge you can read.

Every entry walks the same four steps. No discretionary overrides, no hidden signals — the bot acts only when the structure confirms.

01 — ACCUMULATION

Price coils

The bot detects when price compresses into a tight range relative to recent volatility — energy building before a move.

02 — MANIPULATION

The sweep

A sharp wick pushes beyond the range, trapping breakout traders and hunting stops — the liquidity grab before the real move.

03 — DISTRIBUTION

FVG confirms

A Fair Value Gap forms in the opposite direction, confirming intent. The bot enters at the FVG close — not on hope.

04 — EXECUTION

2:1 every time

Stop loss sits beyond the manipulation extreme (volatility-based). Take profit at 2× the stop distance. Wins pay double.
